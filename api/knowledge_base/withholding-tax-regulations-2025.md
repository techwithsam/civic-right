# Withholding Tax Regulations & Small Business Relief Order 2025
**Issuing Authority**: Federal Ministry of Finance & Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)  
**Topic**: Tax Reform  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-07-01  
**Official URL**: https://firs.gov.ng/regulations/withholding-tax-2025  

## 1. Complete Exemption for Small and Micro Businesses
To curb working-capital depletion among small contractors, vendors, and service providers, the Withholding Tax (WHT) Regulations 2025 introduce a complete exemption for transactions carried out by small enterprises.

### Provisions:
- **No Withholding Deductions on Invoices**: Corporate clients and government agencies are strictly prohibited from deducting Withholding Tax on invoice payments made to registered small businesses with turnover below ₦50 million.
- **Manufacturers and Agricultural Input Relief**: Domestic manufacturing operations, agricultural supply contracts, and food transport services enjoy 0% WHT to suppress supply-chain inflation.
- **Penalty for Unlawful Deduction**: Any corporation or procurement unit that unlawfully deducts WHT from an exempt small enterprise must refund the deducted sum within 14 working days or incur statutory interest penalties.
- **Documentation Needed**: A small vendor simply provides their valid Tax Identification Number (TIN) and a standardized statutory declaration of turnover to their client.
