# Mandatory Capping Order on Estimated Billing for Unmetered Customers
**Issuing Authority**: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC)  
**Topic**: Electricity  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-05-12  
**Official URL**: https://nerc.gov.ng/orders/estimated-billing-caps  

## 1. Ceiling on Monthly Unmetered Charges
Under NERC's revised Capping of Estimated Bills Order, distribution companies are legally prohibited from issuing outrageous, arbitrary, or unverified estimated electricity bills to residential and commercial customers awaiting prepaid meters.

### Mandatory Rules:
- **Maximum Allowable kWh**: NERC publishes monthly capping orders specifying the maximum kilowatt-hours (kWh) a DisCo can bill unmetered customers on each specific distribution transformer or feeder.
- **Right to Reject Inflated Bills**: If an estimated bill exceeds the NERC published monthly cap for that customer's feeder, the customer is legally entitled to pay only the capped amount and contest the excess.
- **Disconnected Status Exemption**: Any unmetered premises that suffers an outage lasting more than 15 consecutive days in a billing cycle must receive a proportional bill reduction.
- **Forced Disconnection Barred**: DisCos may not disconnect an unmetered customer who tenders payment up to the published NERC cap while awaiting meter installation.
