# National Flood Early Warning Advisory & Municipal Drainage Maintenance Regulations
**Issuing Authority**: Federal Ministry of Environment & Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (NIHSA)  
**Topic**: Infrastructure  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-05-30  
**Official URL**: https://environment.gov.ng/advisories/flood-prevention-2025  

## 1. Flood Prevention and Drainage Clearance Obligations
The Federal Ministry of Environment, in partnership with state environmental protection agencies, outlines statutory flood mitigation obligations for municipal governments, property developers, and residential communities.

### Key Directives:
- **Annual Pre-Rain Drainage Dredging**: State Ministries of Environment and local councils must clear and desilt primary stormwater channels, culverts, and canal basins prior to peak rainy seasons (April to October).
- **Prohibition of Waste Dumping**: Dumping domestic solid waste, construction debris, or plastics into street drainage gutters or streams is an environmental crime punishable under municipal sanitation bylaws by fines, community labor, or court prosecution.
- **Setback Encroachment**: Erecting permanent residential or commercial structures on natural drainage right-of-ways or floodplains violates urban planning codes and subjects the structures to immediate demolition without compensation.
- **Reporting Clogged Culverts**: Residents must report overflowing drainage channels and collapsed culvert slabs to local council environmental health officers or civic tech hotlines for emergency desilting.
