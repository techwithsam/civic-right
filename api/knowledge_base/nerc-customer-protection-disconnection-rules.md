# Customer Protection Regulations on Supply Disconnection & Dispute Rights
**Issuing Authority**: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC)  
**Topic**: Electricity  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-03-22  
**Official URL**: https://nerc.gov.ng/regulations/disconnection-rights  

## 1. Disconnection Procedure and Mandatory Notice
Under the NERC Customer Protection Regulations, an electricity distribution company cannot disconnect a customer's power supply arbitrarily or without following statutory due process.

### Regulatory Requirements:
- **Written Notice Period**: The DisCo must provide a written disconnection notice at least ten (10) working days before the scheduled disconnection date.
- **Disputed Bills Exemption**: If a customer has formally lodged a complaint or dispute regarding a bill with the DisCo or NERC Forum Office, the DisCo is barred from disconnecting the premises pending dispute resolution, provided the customer continues paying undisputed average consumption.
- **Prohibition of Collective Neighborhood Disconnections**: DisCos are strictly prohibited from disconnecting an entire transformer, street, or community simply because some residents owe outstanding bills. Mass disconnection without prior regulatory approval attracts heavy financial penalties.
- **Wrongful Disconnection Compensation**: In cases of unlawful disconnection, the DisCo must reconnect the customer within 24 hours and pay regulatory compensation credit directly into the customer's account.
