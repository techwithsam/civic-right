# Public Road Damage Response Charter and Pothole Remediation SLA

## 1. Rapid Intervention Response Targets
The Federal Emergency Road Maintenance Agency (FERMA) Citizens' Service Charter establishes enforceable service level agreements (SLAs) for remediating hazardous potholes and failed asphalt sections along designated economic transport corridors.

### Service Level Agreements:
- **Emergency Inspection**: Within 72 hours of receiving a verified citizen or road user incident report via formal civic tech channels or the FERMA field office, a field engineer is dispatched to survey the failed road section.
- **Asphalt Patching Timeline**: Critical potholes on high-density transit corridors (depth exceeding 100mm) are prioritized for cold-mix or hot-mix asphalt patching within 14 working days of assessment.
- **Washout and Culvert Failures**: Severe road washouts caused by flash floods receive immediate deployment of emergency retaining gabions, stone pitching, and warning barricades within 48 hours to avert fatal vehicular crashes.
- **Public Reporting**: Citizens are encouraged to capture clear geographic coordinates and photos when reporting road failures to speed up crew mobilization.
