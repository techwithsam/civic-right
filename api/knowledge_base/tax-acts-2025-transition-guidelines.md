# General Guidelines for Implementation of Tax Acts 2025
**Issuing Authority**: Federal Ministry of Finance & Presidential Fiscal Policy and Tax Reforms Committee  
**Topic**: Tax Reform  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-06-18  
**Official URL**: https://finance.gov.ng  

## 1. Small Business Exemption Thresholds
Under the 2025 Tax Acts transition framework, micro and small enterprises with an annual gross turnover of ₦50,000,000 (Fifty Million Naira) or less are explicitly exempt from Companies Income Tax (CIT) and Capital Gains Tax. 

### Key Provisions for Small Businesses:
- **Zero Company Income Tax**: Qualifying small enterprises with turnover below ₦50m pay 0% CIT.
- **VAT Exemption**: Small businesses below this threshold are relieved from the obligation to charge, collect, or remit Value Added Tax (VAT) on retail sales of ordinary goods.
- **Simplified Filing**: Small businesses are only required to submit an annual single-page electronic tax return using their Tax Identification Number (TIN) or National Identification Number (NIN).
- **Relief Period**: The transition guidelines apply across all 36 States and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT) to protect employment generation and ease economic pressure on grassroots traders and artisans.

## 2. Threshold for Medium and Large Companies
Enterprises with annual turnover exceeding ₦50,000,000 are classified as medium or large companies and must file standard audited returns with the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) at statutory corporate rates.
