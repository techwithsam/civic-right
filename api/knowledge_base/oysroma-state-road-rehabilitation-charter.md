## 1. Zero-Pothole Campaign and State Road Network Maintenance
The Oyo State Road Maintenance Agency (OYSROMA) is tasked with continuous rehabilitation, emergency patching, and erosion protection across state roads in Ibadan, Oyo, Ogbomoso, Iseyin, and Saki.

### Operational Framework:
- **Rapid Intervention SLA**: Upon receiving citizen complaints regarding failed pavement or potholes on state roads (e.g. Ring Road, Secretariat Road, Dugbe, Mokola, Iwo Road, Elebu), OYSROMA technical teams conduct site validation within 48 to 72 hours.
- **Asphalt Patching & Grading**: Prioritized repairs using asphaltic concrete or stone base grading are executed within 7 to 10 working days of inspection, weather permitting.
- **Drainage Culvert Repair**: Roads damaged by water pooling receive culvert de-silting and drain widening before asphalt re-surfacing.
- **Community Liaison**: Residents and community development associations (CDAs) are urged to log reports through Civic-Right or directly to the OYSROMA complaint desk at the State Secretariat, Agodi, Ibadan.
