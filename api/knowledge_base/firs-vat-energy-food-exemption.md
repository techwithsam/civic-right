# VAT Modification Order on Staple Foods, Public Transit and Clean Energy
**Issuing Authority**: Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) & Federal Ministry of Finance  
**Topic**: Tax Reform  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-08-20  
**Official URL**: https://firs.gov.ng  

## 1. Zero-Rating and Exemption for Essential Household Expenditures
Pursuant to statutory powers under the Value Added Tax Act and executive orders, the Federal Government mandates a 0% VAT rate and full exemption on essential items to alleviate the cost of living.

### Fully Exempt Goods & Services:
- **Basic Food Items**: Locally produced grains, rice, beans, tubers (yam, cassava), fresh meat, fish, eggs, dairy, vegetables, and edible oils are zero-rated. Supermarkets and food distributors may not apply VAT to these items.
- **Public Transportation**: Intra-city commercial bus services, passenger trains, and scheduled public commuter routes are exempt from VAT.
- **Renewable Energy Equipment**: Solar photovoltaic panels, solar inverters, deep-cycle solar batteries, and CNG conversion kits for commercial passenger vehicles attract zero import duties and zero VAT.
- **Consumer Remedies**: Retailers charging VAT on basic food or energy conversion items are liable to civil sanctions and restitution orders. Citizens should demand itemized receipts to challenge improper charges.
