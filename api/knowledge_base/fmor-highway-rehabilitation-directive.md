# Federal Highway Emergency Rehabilitation & Arterial Jurisdiction Framework
**Issuing Authority**: Federal Ministry of Works  
**Topic**: Infrastructure  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-02-15  
**Official URL**: https://works.gov.ng  

## 1. Classification of Road Network Responsibilities
To resolve jurisdictional confusion between federal, state, and local government councils, the Federal Ministry of Works maintains clear boundaries regarding road maintenance and capital rehabilitation.

### Jurisdictional Division:
- **Trunk A & Inter-State Highways**: All inter-state dual carriage ways, expressways (e.g. Lagos–Ibadan, Ibadan–Ilorin, Abuja–Kaduna, Benin–Asaba), and interstate bridges fall under direct Federal Ministry of Works mandate.
- **Trunk B State Arterials**: State trunk roads connecting towns within a state are maintained by State Ministries of Works and State Road Maintenance Agencies (e.g. OYSROMA in Oyo State, LASMRA in Lagos).
- **Access and Feeder Roads**: Internal residential streets, layout roads, and market passages fall under Local Government Area (LGA) works departments.
- **Emergency Interventions on Federal Roads**: Where severe erosion or bridge subsidence threatens transit on a federal road, state governments may execute emergency repairs upon formal notification to the Federal Controller of Works to secure federal reimbursement.
