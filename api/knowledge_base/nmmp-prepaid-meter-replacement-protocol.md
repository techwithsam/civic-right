# National Mass Metering Programme (NMMP) Free Meter Replacement Protocol
**Issuing Authority**: Federal Ministry of Power & NERC  
**Topic**: Electricity  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-06-05  
**Official URL**: https://power.gov.ng  

## 1. Replacement of Faulty and Obsolete Prepaid Meters
Under the joint directives of the Federal Ministry of Power and NERC, electricity customers whose prepaid meters develop faults (such as burnt terminal blocks, token generation errors, STS rollover issues, or firmware defects) are entitled to free technical evaluation and replacement.

### Standards & Obligations:
- **No Direct Payments**: DisCos and third-party contractors are strictly forbidden from demanding that customers pay out-of-pocket for replacing meters damaged by network surges or normal wear and tear.
- **5-Day Resolution SLA**: DisCos must inspect and determine the status of a reported faulty meter within five (5) working days of receiving a customer complaint ticket.
- **Credit Migration**: When a new meter is installed, any existing unutilized energy tokens or credit balance from the old meter must be transferred to the new meter within forty-eight (48) hours.
- **Bypass and Tampering Penalties**: Consumers caught bypassing meters or running illegal direct hook-ups face disconnection, back-billing, and prosecution under the Electricity Act 2023.
