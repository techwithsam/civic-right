# Harmonized Taxes and Prohibition of Road Haulage Levies Notice
**Issuing Authority**: Joint Tax Board (JTB) & National Economic Council  
**Topic**: Tax Reform  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-09-14  
**Official URL**: https://jtb.gov.ng/harmonization-charter  

## 1. Ban on Illegal Roadblocks and Haulage Extortion
The Joint Tax Board (JTB), representing the revenue authorities of all 36 States and the FCT, strictly prohibits the mounting of roadblocks, checkpoints, and non-state revenue task forces collecting multiple road taxes, transit levies, and loading fees along inter-state and intra-state transport corridors.

### Enforceable Regulations:
- **Consolidated Single Haulage Fee**: Transporters of agricultural produce, manufactured goods, and livestock pay only a single consolidated state sticker fee at the point of initial departure.
- **Prohibition of Non-State Enforcers**: No local council, transport union (such as NURTW/RTEAN), or unauthorized revenue consultant has legal authority to impound commercial vehicles or demand cash road tolls.
- **Reporting Channels**: Drivers and business owners subjected to illegal road tax harassment can report directly to the Joint Revenue Monitoring Taskforce via SMS/Hotline or through their State Internal Revenue Service complaint desk.
- **Enforcement Action**: Security agencies (Nigeria Police Force and NSCDC) are mandated to dismantle illegal revenue collection points immediately and prosecute violators.
