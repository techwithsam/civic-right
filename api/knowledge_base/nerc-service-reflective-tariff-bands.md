# Multi-Year Tariff Order (MYTO) Service-Based Tariff Band Classification
**Issuing Authority**: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC)  
**Topic**: Electricity  
**Source Type**: Official Government Publication  
**Publication Date**: 2025-04-03  
**Official URL**: https://nerc.gov.ng  

## 1. Service-Based Tariff (SBT) Feeder Bands
NERC mandates that all electricity distribution companies (DisCos including IBEDC, EKEDC, IKEDC, AEDC, etc.) must charge end-users strictly based on actual hours of electricity supplied per feeder line.

### Band Minimum Daily Supply Hours:
- **Band A**: Minimum of 20 hours per day (measured continuously across a rolling 7-day average).
- **Band B**: Minimum of 16 to 20 hours per day.
- **Band C**: Minimum of 12 to 16 hours per day.
- **Band D**: Minimum of 8 to 12 hours per day.
- **Band E**: Minimum of 4 to 8 hours per day.

## 2. Downward Reclassification & DisCo Penalty
If a DisCo fails to meet the minimum threshold on a classified feeder (for instance, Band A customers receiving less than 20 hours daily for two consecutive weeks), the DisCo must automatically:
1. Reclassify the feeder down to the lower tier (e.g. Band B or C).
2. Refund or credit affected customers for any excess tariff charged during the failure period.
3. Post the feeder supply logs publicly on its website for community verification.
